PhoneDeck 1.5.1: a small maintenance release.

The download section of the README now shows the current version and the installer's size. Nothing else changed: if you're on 1.5.0, PhoneDeck offers this update on its own and installs it for you.

The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. PhoneDeck-Setup-1.5.1.exe.sha256 holds the installer's SHA-256 checksum.
